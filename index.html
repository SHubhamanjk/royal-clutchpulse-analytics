📐 The Master Pressure Formula & Analytical Derivation
P(St) = [ T(St) × Lnorm(St) ]1/2 = √(T(St) × Lnorm(St))

Every delivery is evaluated on a continuous scale P(St) ∈ [0, 100], formalized as the orthogonal geometric intersection between tactical difficulty and match consequence.

1. Tactical Difficulty: T(St)
Formulation:
T(St) = σ(β0 + β1(RRR - Par) + β2(WktsLost / BallsLeft)) × 100

Physical Meaning:
Measures immediate task steepness. Needing 14.0 RRR with 7 wickets down yields near-peak difficulty (T > 90). Needing 4.0 RRR with 9 wickets in hand yields minimal difficulty (T < 10).
2. Consequence Leverage: Lnorm(St)
Formulation:
L(St) = Σ po |WP(St+1(o)) - WP(St)|
Lnorm = ECDF186k(L(St)) × 100

Physical Meaning:
Measures match balance volatility across all 9 possible delivery outcomes, calibrated against empirical distributions across 186,780 deliveries to yield true percentile leverage.
3. Geometric Mean Multiplicative Gate
Formulation:
P = [ T × Lnorm ]1/2

The Orthogonal Gating Law:
If either difficulty or consequence tends toward zero, overall pressure collapses to zero.
• High consequence during a routine chase? Pressure collapses.
• Impossible target when the match is already lost? Pressure collapses.
Eliminates artificial stat inflation in decided contests.
